\smallskip
\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{0.65em}
    \item An offline corpus of \textbf{2.77 million decomposed ideas}.
    \item Each tuple contains problem, challenge, gap, intuition, and proposal.
    \item Papers from 1986 to June 18, 2026; reference checks and topic-relevant method filtering.
    \item \textbf{Inputs:} Problem definition and challenge.
    \item \textbf{Three targets:} Methodological gap, research intuition, and complete proposal.
